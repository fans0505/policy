AI Content and Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

This Policy explains how Mosaic uses AI to generate pixel creations and how users must use the generation feature safely and responsibly.

1. How AI Is Used

  1. Mosaic does not ask the model to generate an image directly. A description is converted into a structured palette and a 16×16, 24×24, or 32×32 pixel grid, then rendered on the device as PNG files and animation frames.
  2. Generation requests are sent through Mosaic's AIService to the DeepSeek API.
  3. AI output is probabilistic and may contain errors, repetition, unexpected styles, or results that do not fully match the description.
  4. Mosaic performs structural and basic safety checks but cannot guarantee that every output is appropriate for every setting.

2. Before You Submit Content

  • Do not enter names, contact details, government identifiers, account information, precise location, health information, or other sensitive personal information.
  • Do not submit private information, likeness details, trade secrets, or substantial details from protected works unless you have permission.
  • If a description involves a real person, brand, or character, make sure your use is lawful and respects relevant rights.
  • Generated content is a creative aid and must not be used as the basis for medical, legal, financial, educational-assessment, or personal-safety decisions.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

You must not request or use Mosaic to create:

  • Sexual exploitation or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Content promoting hatred, harassment, violent extremism, self-harm, or real-world injury.
  • Fraud, impersonation, malicious defamation, privacy violations, or unlawful surveillance.
  • Content infringing copyright, trademark, likeness, trade-secret, or other rights.
  • Attempts to evade safety controls, attack the service, automate quota abuse, or otherwise misuse Mosaic.

System instructions favor cute, friendly, expressive characters and reject explicit or clearly harmful directions.

4. Output and Rights

  1. Output may not be unique, and other users may receive similar results.
  2. You are responsible for deciding whether output is suitable for your intended use.
  3. Conduct an appropriate rights review before publishing, commercializing, or combining output with a third-party brand.
  4. Do not continue to distribute or use clearly inappropriate output.

5. Consent to Data Processing

Before the first AI generation, Mosaic explains what data will be sent and who receives it. You may decline or withdraw consent in Settings. After withdrawal, no new generation request will be sent, but existing local creations remain available to view or delete.

6. Enforcement and Feedback

The developer may block prohibited requests, limit anomalous calls, or disable access associated with an abusive installation.
