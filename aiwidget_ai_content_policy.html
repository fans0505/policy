Content Guidelines

Mosika Pets · Effective October 22, 2026

Create responsibly

Mosika Pets turns your description into pixel artwork. Only submit material you are entitled to use. Avoid private information, real people’s sensitive attributes, or confidential material.

Prohibited content

Do not request or share sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, threats, instructions for wrongdoing, impersonation intended to deceive, or content that infringes privacy or intellectual property rights. Do not attempt to bypass safety measures.

Review before sharing

Generated characters can be inaccurate, unexpected, or similar to existing works. Review every result before using or sharing it. The App does not guarantee exclusive ownership, originality, or suitability for a particular commercial use. Obtain any permissions needed for third-party names, likenesses, characters, or brands.

Report a problem

Email support with the relevant description and result, the approximate time, and an explanation of your concern. Remove private information before sending. We review reports and may restrict harmful or abusive requests. Delete unwanted local creations from the collection.
