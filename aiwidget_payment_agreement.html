Payment Agreement

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

This Payment Agreement applies to auto-renewing subscriptions and consumable generation credit packs offered in Mosaic through Apple In-App Purchase.

1. Free Usage

  1. A user without a subscription currently receives three free generations each day.
  2. Free usage resets according to the device's local date, does not roll over, cannot be transferred, and has no cash value.
  3. The entitlement shown in the App at the time of use controls.

2. Auto-Renewing Subscriptions

  1. Mosaic offers monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions.
  2. While active, a subscription provides unlimited AI generation, subject to reasonable technical capacity, safety controls, and the AI Content and Acceptable Use Policy.
  3. Price, currency, billing period, and any trial or promotion are shown in the purchase confirmation interface.
  4. Apple charges your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  5. The subscription automatically renews for the selected period unless auto-renewal is turned off in your Apple Account subscription settings.
  6. Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current paid period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  7. Apple handles price-change notices, billing retries, refunds, and revocations under its applicable rules.

3. Generation Credit Packs

  1. Mosaic may offer consumable packs of 50, 200, or 600 generations. Final quantity and price are shown in the purchase interface.
  2. One generation is consumed when a newly generated creation is successfully saved. A failed or incomplete save should not consume a generation.
  3. Purchased generations do not expire on a calendar date and cannot be transferred, redeemed for cash, or combined across Apple Accounts.
  4. Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases and are generally not restored by Apple's Restore Purchases process.
  5. In the current version, the credit-pack balance is associated with this App installation and stored locally. Uninstalling the App, erasing device data, or changing devices may make the balance unrecoverable.

Please consider the local-storage limitation before purchasing a generation credit pack.

4. Billing, Refunds, and Revocations

  1. All payments are processed and settled by Apple In-App Purchase.
  2. Refund requests and decisions are handled through Apple's official channels.
  3. If Apple revokes or refunds a transaction, the developer may remove the corresponding unused entitlement as permitted by law.

5. Restore and Manage Purchases

  1. You may use Restore Purchases in Mosaic to recover restorable subscription entitlements.
  2. You may manage or cancel auto-renewing subscriptions from the Subscriptions page of your Apple Account.

6. Service Interruptions

Brief maintenance, network failures, or third-party AI-service outages do not automatically create a right to a refund. This section does not limit statutory consumer rights or refund rights provided by Apple.

7. Changes

The developer may change plans, prices, and promotions offered in the future but will not retroactively reduce an active paid subscription period or generation credits already delivered, except where required by law or Apple platform rules.
