MazeLume Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

In short: MazeLume does not collect personal data, show ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.

About MazeLume

MazeLume is a single-player 3D maze game. It does not require an account and does not offer in-app purchases.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide gameplay and remember your progress, MazeLume stores the following information locally on your device:

  • App preferences
  • Your current game
  • Level completion status and completion dates

Optional iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud for MazeLume, Apple SwiftData and CloudKit may sync completion events and completed-level records through your private iCloud database. This feature is optional. The developer cannot access or receive those private records.

Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy terms. You can use MazeLume without iCloud sync.

What We Do Not Collect

MazeLume has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive your gameplay records, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information through the App.

Sharing and Sale of Data

Because the developer does not collect your data, we do not sell it or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Your Choices

You can use the Reset option in MazeLume to clear saved game progress. You can also disable iCloud for MazeLume in your device settings. Removing the App deletes its locally stored data; records already synced to your private iCloud database are managed through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

MazeLume does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If MazeLume's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
