Support

Timekin · Effective October 25, 2026

An interrupted request or missing result

Reopen the App and let it check any saved local job or preview before starting again. A saved result or checkpoint may be continued on this device without counting the same completed job again. There is no cloud result recovery. If no local result remains, contact support about an allowance problem; avoid uninstalling content you want to keep. Starting a deliberate new request or creation uses a new allowance. Confirmed technical failures release their local reservation.

When contacting us, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Never send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Plans, schedules, and your assistant

Create and edit tasks, plans, courses, or habits locally. Open the assistant from the App when you want help. Online planning and chat need an internet connection. Always review a proposed change before accepting it.

Planning or chat is not responding

Check your connection and reopen Timekin. If a request is still running, allow it time to finish. Retry after a short wait if the service is unavailable. If the issue persists, send the approximate time, App version, and steps to support.

Voice input

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition access for Timekin in iOS Settings. Voice recognition availability depends on the selected language, device, and network. You can always type instead. Review transcribed text before sending it.

Calendar and Reminders sync

Grant the relevant permission in iOS Settings, then use Timekin’s sync action. The App uses its dedicated calendar or reminders. Revoking permission stops future access but does not automatically delete items already saved in the system apps.

Notifications and Widgets

Enable notifications in iOS Settings if you want reminders, and check Focus settings. For a stale Widget, open Timekin once and wait for the system refresh. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS.

Deleting data

The App’s planning-data deletion action does not also delete chat history or planning drafts. App removal removes local documents, so preserve anything you need first. Calendar events, reminders, and system backups may remain outside the App. Contact us for help with privacy requests.

Membership and allowance

Free access includes 10 scheduling requests per month and one planning stage to try once. Timekin Plus includes 150 scheduling requests and 8 planning stages or updates per month. These are separate allowances; 8 stages does not mean 8 complete multi-stage goals. One scheduling request counts once when delivered. A new request, a new planning stage or a deliberate redo uses another allowance. Confirmed technical failures and planning results rejected as unusable before delivery do not count; uncertain requests may need reconciliation. Saved plans, manual edits and existing local tools remain available without Plus.

Apple charges your Apple Account and renews the subscription unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe; signed purchase records are checked before access is restored. Cancellation normally preserves access until the paid period ends, subject to Apple’s refund or revocation status.

For an Apple-billed charge, use Apple’s refund support. A refund request is separate from canceling a subscription. Contact us with the product name, purchase date and a description of any missing allowance; never send passwords or payment credentials. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights. Availability of an offering depends on your App version, region and App Store availability; this website does not complete purchases.

Usage is counted separately on each device and is not synchronized across devices. Restore Purchases restores membership eligibility, not a fresh allowance or another device’s usage history. Switching between monthly and annual plans does not reset an already-used allowance month. An unused one-time free trial is available only on the free plan; it is not added to Plus and remains available after Plus expires, while its local record is retained.
