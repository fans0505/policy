Jelly Bonk Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

In short: Jelly Bonk does not collect personal data, show ads, track you, require an account, or use third-party analytics. Everything stays on your device.

About Jelly Bonk

Jelly Bonk is a single-player auto-battle game. It does not require an account and does not offer in-app purchases.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide gameplay and remember your progress, Jelly Bonk stores the following information locally on your device:

  • App preferences (sound and haptics settings)
  • Your current jelly and skill loadout
  • Level completion status, stars and unlocks
  • Codex collection progress

This data never leaves your device. Jelly Bonk has no cloud sync, no online services and no servers.

What We Do Not Collect

Jelly Bonk has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive your gameplay records, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information through the App.

Sharing and Sale of Data

Because the developer does not collect your data, we do not sell it or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Your Choices

Removing the App deletes its locally stored data. There is nothing else to manage, because nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Jelly Bonk is a cartoon game with no age-restricted content and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If Jelly Bonk's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
