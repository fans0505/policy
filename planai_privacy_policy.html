Privacy Policy

Timekin · Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Timekin and its developer handle information when you use the App and its online features.

You can use local features without sending content for online generation. Online replies and creations require processing of the content described below. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or other people’s private information.

Planning data saved on your device

Timekin stores plans, tasks, schedules, courses, habits, check-ins, planning drafts, chat conversations and messages on your device. Preferences and focus-timer state are also local. A small local summary is shared with the App’s Widgets. The App does not provide an account-based cloud library or iCloud sync.

Online processing & service providers

When you request help, your input and relevant conversation are sent for online processing. Planning requests may include goals, dates, time budgets, constraints, progress, and feedback. The assistant can read relevant plans, tasks, courses, or schedules to answer your request, and send the relevant results as context. Your entire device calendar is not automatically uploaded.

Requests pass through our service hosted by Cloudflare and OpenRouter. Text requests use available providers of DeepSeek or GLM services; the executing provider may vary with routing and availability. OpenRouter publishes its provider directory and data-handling policies. Only the content needed for your request is sent; these services do not receive unrestricted access to your device. Processing may occur outside your country. Each provider’s policies and applicable service terms govern its own retention and handling.

Permissions & purchases

Microphone and speech-recognition access are optional for dictation. Apple may process audio for recognition according to system settings; Timekin does not save the original recording or send it to its online assistant. Transcribed text follows the same rules as text you type. Calendar and Reminders access is used for the App’s dedicated events or reminders when you choose to sync. Notifications are local and optional. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Service access, security & support

We process anonymous app or device identifiers, service-access records, request times, usage counts, and diagnostic information to provide access, prevent misuse, and investigate failures. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and network information. Our operational logs do not include your message text or generated content. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use cross-app tracking or third-party advertising analytics in the App.

Retention & deletion

In My Space → Preferences & data, choosing Delete All Data deletes Timekin’s local plans, tasks, schedules, courses, habit check-ins, chat conversations and messages, and planning drafts. The App also attempts to clear focus-timer state, Widget content, and App-managed reminders. If part of the cleanup cannot finish, the App reports the result so you can follow up. This does not cancel Apple subscriptions, reset membership allowance records, or automatically erase records held by service providers, Apple, or system backups. App removal deletes local documents, while secure device credentials and system backups may remain under system controls. Events or reminders still present in system apps can be managed there. Contact us for help with privacy requests.

The online service does not provide a saved cloud library of your content. This does not mean all online processing has zero retention: service-access records, usage counts, security records, and provider-held data may be retained as needed for service operation, security, legal obligations, or the provider’s applicable terms. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of data we hold; we may need limited information to locate an anonymous record. Deleting local content does not automatically erase records held by service providers or Apple.

Your choices & privacy rights

Before you first share content for online AI processing, Timekin explains what will be sent, the purpose, and the third-party services, and asks you to explicitly allow sharing. You can decline and continue using manual planning, scheduling, saved records, focus timers, and other local tools. Change your choice in My Space → Preferences & data → AI data sharing. Turning sharing off blocks subsequent online AI requests; it cannot retrieve data already sent or automatically delete provider-held records. If the sharing details materially change, the App asks for permission again.

You can also change available system permissions in iOS Settings. You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, a copy, or a restriction or objection where these rights apply. You may also complain to your local privacy authority. We respond as required by applicable law and do not deny local features for exercising privacy rights.

Security, children & updates

We use encrypted connections and limit processing to the service’s purposes. No system guarantees complete security. The App is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required locally. Minors should involve a parent or guardian. Contact us if a child’s personal data has been submitted. We may update this policy as the product changes; the effective date identifies this version.

These legal and support pages use GitHub Pages. Opening them sends ordinary web-request information to the hosting provider. The pages do not add advertising cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts.

Apple purchases and device-local allowances

Apple handles payments. The App uses verified StoreKit purchase records to determine your subscription and expiry on this device. Product, transaction, subscription-period and expiry information may be kept locally for this purpose. Purchase proofs are not sent to our generation service for membership verification. We do not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details.

Allowances and creation/request jobs are recorded on this device, including quota periods, operation type, reservations, completion or failure state and a result checksum. The App saves these records to prevent counting the same job twice. They are not a shared account balance and do not sync between devices. Restore Purchases can restore an eligible Apple subscription; it does not reset this device’s usage or retrieve usage records from another device. App removal or device erasure can remove local records; system backups may retain copies under system controls. Our service does not provide a membership cloud ledger or cloud result-recovery library.
