Purchase Terms

Timequill · Effective October 25, 2026

Membership and allowance

Free access allows up to 5 user questions per day and 100 per month. Timequill Plus includes 600 user questions per month, shared across individual and group conversations. One question counts once even if several characters respond. Starting a new question or asking again uses another question. Confirmed technical failures do not consume a question; an uncertain request may need to be checked before its allowance is released. Local browsing, offline investigations and saved conversations remain available without Plus.

Usage is counted separately on each device and is not synchronized across devices. Restore Purchases restores membership eligibility, not a fresh allowance or another device’s usage history. Switching between monthly and annual plans does not reset an already-used allowance month.

Subscriptions and renewal

Monthly and annual Plus plans provide the same monthly allowance. Annual allowances refresh one month at a time, not as an entire year in advance. Unused allowance does not roll over or convert between categories. Refresh times and available allowance are shown in the App. Localized prices, currency and the billing period are shown before Apple confirms a purchase.

Apple charges your Apple Account and renews the subscription unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe; signed purchase records are checked before access is restored. Cancellation normally preserves access until the paid period ends, subject to Apple’s refund or revocation status.

Restoring, cancellation and refunds

For an Apple-billed charge, use Apple’s refund support. A refund request is separate from canceling a subscription. Contact us with the product name, purchase date and a description of any missing allowance; never send passwords or payment credentials. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights. Availability of an offering depends on your App version, region and App Store availability; this website does not complete purchases.
