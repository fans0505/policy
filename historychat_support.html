Help with Timequill. Browse the answers below or email us for product, privacy, or content support.

When contacting us, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Never send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Start a conversation

Choose a historical character, open their conversation, and send a question. Character browsing, portraits, and saved conversations are available locally; new replies need an internet connection.

A reply does not arrive

Check your connection, reopen Timequill, and retry after a short wait. If the service has reached a usage limit, wait before trying again. Repeatedly tapping Send will not help. Include the approximate time and steps in your support email if the problem continues.

A historical answer seems wrong

Characters are generated interpretations and may make factual mistakes. Compare important claims with reliable historical sources. To report a problematic answer, send the character name, your question, and the relevant reply after removing private information.

Saved conversations and cache

Conversations are stored on this device. Delete a conversation from the conversation list when you no longer need it. Clearing the cache does not delete conversations. There is no account-based cross-device recovery, so avoid uninstalling the App to troubleshoot a conversation you want to keep.

Reading aloud

Turn up the device’s media volume and check your audio output. The available voices and their pronunciation depend on the device’s speech settings. No voice recording is required.

Billing

The current version has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. For a charge from an older version, check your Apple receipt and use Apple’s refund support.
