Support

Timequill · Effective October 25, 2026

An interrupted request or missing result

Reopen the App and let it check any saved local job or preview before starting again. A saved result or checkpoint may be continued on this device without counting the same completed job again. There is no cloud result recovery. If no local result remains, contact support about an allowance problem; avoid uninstalling content you want to keep. Starting a deliberate new request or creation uses a new allowance. Confirmed technical failures release their local reservation.

Help with Timequill. Browse the answers below or email us for product, privacy, or content support.

When contacting us, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Never send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Start a conversation

Choose a historical character, open their conversation, and send a question. Character browsing, portraits, and saved conversations are available locally; new replies need an internet connection.

A reply does not arrive

Check your connection, reopen Timequill, and retry after a short wait. If the service has reached a usage limit, wait before trying again. Repeatedly tapping Send will not help. Include the approximate time and steps in your support email if the problem continues.

A historical answer seems wrong

Characters are generated interpretations and may make factual mistakes. Compare important claims with reliable historical sources. To report a problematic answer, send the character name, your question, and the relevant reply after removing private information.

Saved conversations and cache

Conversations are stored on this device. Delete a conversation from the conversation list when you no longer need it. Clearing the cache does not delete conversations. There is no account-based cross-device recovery, so avoid uninstalling the App to troubleshoot a conversation you want to keep.

Reading aloud

Turn up the device’s media volume and check your audio output. The available voices and their pronunciation depend on the device’s speech settings. No voice recording is required.

Membership and allowance

Free access allows up to 5 user questions per day and 100 per month. Timequill Plus includes 600 user questions per month, shared across individual and group conversations. One question counts once even if several characters respond. Starting a new question or asking again uses another question. Confirmed technical failures do not consume a question; an uncertain request may need to be checked before its allowance is released. Local browsing, offline investigations and saved conversations remain available without Plus.

Apple charges your Apple Account and renews the subscription unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe; signed purchase records are checked before access is restored. Cancellation normally preserves access until the paid period ends, subject to Apple’s refund or revocation status.

For an Apple-billed charge, use Apple’s refund support. A refund request is separate from canceling a subscription. Contact us with the product name, purchase date and a description of any missing allowance; never send passwords or payment credentials. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights. Availability of an offering depends on your App version, region and App Store availability; this website does not complete purchases.

Usage is counted separately on each device and is not synchronized across devices. Restore Purchases restores membership eligibility, not a fresh allowance or another device’s usage history. Switching between monthly and annual plans does not reset an already-used allowance month.
