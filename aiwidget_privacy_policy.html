Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

The developer of Mosaic (the "App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App and its AI generation features.

1. Information We Process

  1. Creative content: character descriptions, color preferences, pixel size, refinement requests, and an existing character's palette and pixel grid when you request a refinement.
  2. Anonymous installation information: a randomly generated installation identifier and secret, access token, app identifier, and last-access time used for authentication, rate limiting, abuse prevention, and service security.
  3. Purchase and entitlement information: subscription status and transaction results provided by the App Store, plus free usage, credit-pack balance, and unlock state stored on the device.
  4. Local creation data: character names, personalities, palettes, animation frames, and rendered PNG files stored on your device for use by the App and Widget.
  5. Necessary technical information: IP address, request time, API route, status code, and error information may be processed for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

Do not include names, contact details, government identifiers, account information, health information, or another person's private content in a character description.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Generate, validate, and refine pixel characters.
  • Save creations and display them in Widgets on your device.
  • Provide free usage, subscriptions, and credit-pack entitlements.
  • Authenticate installations, apply rate limits, prevent fraud, protect the service, and troubleshoot problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect users, the developer, and the service.

We do not sell personal information, display personalized advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

3. AI Services and Third Parties

  1. When you initiate generation or refinement, relevant creative content is sent to Mosaic's AIService and then to the DeepSeek API to produce structured pixel data.
  2. DeepSeek may temporarily cache request content under its published service design. Unused cache entries are generally cleared automatically within hours to days.
  3. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store and StoreKit. Apple independently handles payments, subscription management, refunds, and required transaction information.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  1. Characters and PNG files remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the App, or erase device data.
  2. Mosaic's AIService currently does not store character descriptions or model output in its business database after a request completes.
  3. Operational logs and anonymous installation records are retained only as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service.
  4. You may delete individual characters in the App, withdraw future AI-processing consent in Settings, or uninstall the App to remove local data.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator. Withdrawing consent prevents future AI generation requests but does not affect existing local creations.

6. Children and Teens

The App is not independently offered to children below the digital-consent age in their location. Minors should use the App only after a parent or guardian has reviewed this Policy.

7. Security

We use transport encryption, Keychain storage, token authentication, data minimization, and access controls. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Policy Updates

This Policy may be updated when features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
