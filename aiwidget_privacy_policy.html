Privacy Policy

Mosika Pets · Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how Mosika Pets and its developer handle information when you use the App and its online features.

You can use local features without sending content for online generation. Generating or refining a character requires processing of the content described below. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or other people’s private information.

Creations saved on your device

Mosika Pets stores character names, personalities, palettes, animation frames, creation dates, rendered images, behavior choices, interaction dates, and shared-moment records on your device. A local shared area makes saved characters and your display choice available to the Widget. The App does not provide an account-based cloud library or iCloud sync. Adopting a bundled companion and interacting with saved companions are local features and do not send generation requests.

Manual companion backups

You can choose to export a companion file containing its name, personality, palette, animation frames, behavior choices, interaction dates, and shared-moment records, then import it on another device. Backups do not include generation balances, purchase entitlements, payment transactions, or authentication credentials. This is a manual file backup, not automatic cloud synchronization. The App does not upload the file to our service. If you use the system share sheet or save it with another provider, the destination you choose handles that copy. Deleting a companion in the App does not delete copies you previously exported; you manage those separately.

Online processing & service providers

When you choose online creation or refinement, the App sends your description, color preferences, requested motion, language and, where applicable, selected pixel size. Image-based animation may first generate a master character image, then upload that image with motion or change instructions for reference-based editing. Legacy pixel refinements also send the existing character's name, personality, palette, animation frames and requested changes. The Widget only displays saved local creations and does not send generation requests.

Requests pass through our online service, hosted by Cloudflare, and OpenRouter, which routes the content to the model service used for the requested feature. Current text services include DeepSeek and GLM (Z.ai); current image generation and reference editing use Black Forest Labs (FLUX) through OpenRouter. The selected services receive the text, reference images and generated results needed for the request, rather than unrestricted access to your device. Processing may occur outside your country. Their published privacy policies and applicable service agreements govern their own handling and retention of data.

Character images, animation & local recovery

Static artwork and image-based characters use the online processing chain above. Reference editing sends the actual master or reference image bytes together with the requested motion or changes. The App receives resulting image bytes through our own online service; it does not separately download the result from a model provider's image-hosting address.

Received master images and generated originals, together with their descriptions, preferences, motion requests and creation dates, may be saved automatically in a local recovery area, including when later editing, animation preparation or cancellation prevents a finished character. You can reopen an available original, retry local preparation, export it, or explicitly delete it in the creation recovery screen. Saving an original does not automatically add it to the Widget. Separate static base-image drafts are saved locally when you choose to save them and can be reopened or deleted from the static artwork screen.

Permissions & purchases

The App does not request access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or precise location. Purchases are handled by Apple. The App processes transaction and entitlement information and stores usage and generation-pack balances to deliver purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Service access, security & support

We process anonymous app or device identifiers, service-access records, request times, usage counts, and diagnostic information to provide access, apply usage limits, prevent misuse, and investigate failures. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and network information. Our operational logs do not include your message text or generated content. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use cross-app tracking or third-party advertising analytics in the App.

Retention & deletion

Use the menu in a companion's details to delete it, remove its local creation, companion history and rendered images, and update the Widget's saved data. Saved creations remain available to view or delete if you stop using online generation and refinement. App removal deletes the App's local documents; system backups and secure device credentials may remain under system controls.

Recovery originals and static artwork drafts are managed separately from saved companions. Deleting a companion does not automatically delete its recovery originals; explicitly delete those in the relevant recovery or draft screen. If you export or share an original, you manage the exported copy at the destination. Deleting the local original does not remove previously exported copies.

The online service does not provide a saved cloud library of your content. This does not mean online processing has zero retention. Service-access, usage and security records, and data held by OpenRouter or model service providers, may be retained for service operation, safety, legal obligations or their applicable terms. Any permitted use for service or model improvement depends on the relevant provider's policy and service agreement; we do not promise zero retention or a blanket no-training rule for all providers. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of data we hold; we may need limited information to locate an anonymous record. Local deletion does not automatically erase records held by service providers or Apple.

Your choices & privacy rights

You can choose not to use online generation and refinement. Stop using those features to avoid further creation requests; existing local features remain available for viewing saved creations, displaying them in Widgets, and deleting them. You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, a copy, or a restriction or objection where these rights apply. You may also complain to your local privacy authority. We respond as required by applicable law and do not deny local features for exercising privacy rights.

Security, children & updates

We use encrypted connections and limit processing to the service’s purposes. No system guarantees complete security. The App is not directed to children under 13 or a higher minimum age required locally. Minors should involve a parent or guardian. Contact us if a child’s personal data has been submitted. We may update this policy as the product changes; the effective date identifies this version.

These legal and support pages use GitHub Pages. Opening them sends ordinary web-request information to the hosting provider. The pages do not add advertising cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts.

静态底图与图像角色使用上述在线处理链路。参考图编辑会上传实际母图或参考图片字节，以及动作或修改要求。App 通过我们的自有在线服务接收结果图片字节，不再单独从模型供应商的图片托管地址下载结果。

已收到的母图、生成原图及其描述、偏好、动作要求与创建日期，可能自动保存到本机恢复区，包括后续编辑、动画整理失败或取消，未形成完整角色的情况。你可在创作恢复页面重新打开已有原图、重试本机整理、导出原图或明确删除。原图保存不会自动加入小组件。独立静态底图草稿仍在你主动保存时写入本机，并可在静态底图页面重新打开或删除。
