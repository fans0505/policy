Help with Mosika Pets. Browse the answers below or email us for product, privacy, or content support.

When contacting us, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Never send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Choose Classic or Custom Motion

Classic creates a companion with free local Still, Bounce or Sway movement. Custom Motion uses the action you describe to create a custom animation. Before starting or creating again, check the displayed creation type and one-use cost. Local movement changes and saving existing results are free.

A creation does not finish

Check your connection, reopen Mosika Pets, and retry after a short wait. Check your remaining usage in the App. If the service is temporarily unavailable, allow time before retrying. Do not uninstall the App to fix a balance issue.

Add or refresh a Widget

Save a character first. On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, use Edit to add a Widget, and choose Mosika Pets. Set the character you want in the App or edit the Widget’s choice where available. If it looks stale, open the App once and wait for iOS to refresh it.

Membership and allowance

Free access includes one Classic creation, recorded once on this device. Mosika Plus includes 20 Classic and 4 Custom Motion creations per month in separate allowances. Classic creates one companion image with local Still, Bounce or Sway movement; Custom Motion uses a requested action to create an animation. A usable generated result counts when it is delivered, before saving. Generated motion, direction or appearance may differ from your instructions. Creating again to obtain a different result is a new creation and uses another allowance. Only technical failures such as no image or an invalid unusable result do not count; uncertain requests are checked before release. Saving, viewing existing companions, Widgets and switching local movements do not consume creations. The current offer has no extra generation packs and is not unlimited.

Apple charges your Apple Account and renews the subscription unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel billing. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe; signed purchase records are checked before access is restored. Cancellation normally preserves access until the paid period ends, subject to Apple’s refund or revocation status.

For an Apple-billed charge, use Apple’s refund support. A refund request is separate from canceling a subscription. Contact us with the product name, purchase date and a description of any missing allowance; never send passwords or payment credentials. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights. Availability of an offering depends on your App version, region and App Store availability; this website does not complete purchases.

Usage is counted separately on each device and is not synchronized across devices. Restore Purchases restores membership eligibility, not a fresh allowance or another device’s usage history. Switching between monthly and annual plans does not reset an already-used allowance month. An unused one-time free trial is available only on the free plan; it is not added to Plus and remains available after Plus expires, while its local record is retained.

An interrupted request or missing result

Reopen the App and let it check any saved local job or preview before starting again. A saved result or checkpoint may be continued on this device without counting the same completed job again. There is no cloud result recovery. If no local result remains, contact support about an allowance problem; avoid uninstalling content you want to keep. Starting a deliberate new request or creation uses a new allowance. Confirmed technical failures release their local reservation.

Privacy and saved creations

Before your first online creation or legacy character refinement, review the data-sharing explanation and choose whether to agree. Declining keeps your creation draft and still lets you adopt bundled companions, view saved creations, interact locally, make manual backups, and use Widgets. Accepting the User Agreement does not enable AI data sharing.

To review or withdraw permission, open Me > Settings > Online creation & privacy. Withdrawal stops subsequent online requests, including the next stage of an ongoing creation, but cannot recall information already sent. A request already sent may finish; valid paid image results already received in full continue through local recovery storage and are not erased by withdrawal. Withdrawing permission does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Delete a character from the collection to remove its local data and images. Recovery originals are managed separately in Recent creations. Saved creations are not backed up to an account-based cloud library. See the Privacy Policy for processing, retention and deletion details.

Report an unsafe result or rights concern

Email us with the character description and relevant result after removing private information. Explain whether the problem involves inappropriate content, another person’s rights, or an unexpected generation.
