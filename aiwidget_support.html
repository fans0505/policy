Help with Mosika Pets. Browse the answers below or email us for product, privacy, or content support.

When contacting us, include your device model, iOS version, App version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first. Never send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Create or refine a companion

Describe the appearance and personality you want, choose a color preference and pixel size, then create a character. For refinements, request one or two clear changes at a time. New creations need an internet connection; saved creations remain local.

A creation does not finish

Check your connection, reopen Mosika Pets, and retry after a short wait. Check your remaining usage in the App. If the service is temporarily unavailable, allow time before retrying. Do not uninstall the App to fix a balance issue.

Add or refresh a Widget

Save a character first. On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, use Edit to add a Widget, and choose Mosika Pets. Set the character you want in the App or edit the Widget’s choice where available. If it looks stale, open the App once and wait for iOS to refresh it.

Restore a purchase or cancel a subscription

Use Restore Purchases in the App with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple’s cancellation guide and refund guide explain the next steps. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Generation-pack balances

Generation packs are consumable purchases. Their balance is stored on this device and may not be recoverable after App removal, device erasure, or switching devices. Contact support before reinstalling if a balance looks wrong. Include the purchase date and product name; never send payment credentials.

Privacy and saved creations

You can withdraw future online-creation permission in Settings. Existing creations remain available. Delete a character from the collection to remove its local data and images. Saved creations are not backed up to an account-based cloud library.

Report an unsafe result or rights concern

Email us with the character description and relevant result after removing private information. Explain whether the problem involves inappropriate content, another person’s rights, or an unexpected generation.

隐私与已存作品

可在设置中撤回后续在线创作授权，已有作品仍可使用。在图鉴中删除角色可移除其本机数据与图片。作品不会备份到基于账号的云端资料库。

反馈不当结果或权利问题

请先移除私人信息，再将角色描述与相关结果发至支持邮箱，并说明是不当内容、涉及他人权利，还是生成结果异常。
