Mosaic Support

Help for AI pixel characters and animated Widgets

Creating or refining a character requires an internet connection. Characters already saved on your device remain available to the App and Widget without generating them again.

Quick Troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen Mosaic.
  2. Check your internet connection, then retry the action.
  3. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  4. Install the latest iOS or iPadOS update available for your device.
  5. Make sure your device has enough free storage for character frames.

AI Generation

If a character fails to generate or does not match your idea:

  1. Use a short, concrete description of the character's appearance and personality.
  2. Add an optional color preference and choose a pixel size appropriate for your Widget.
  3. Use Apply Changes to refine one or two details at a time.
  4. If the service is temporarily unavailable, wait a moment and retry.

Please do not include names, account details, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a prompt.

Widget Setup and Refresh

  1. Save a generated character in Mosaic.
  2. Return to the Home Screen, long-press an empty area, choose Edit, and add the Mosaic Widget.
  3. To display a different character, set it as the default in Mosaic or use Edit Widget when that option is available.
  4. If a Widget does not refresh, open Mosaic once, confirm the character still exists, and wait for the next system Widget refresh.

Subscriptions and Generation Packs

  1. Use Restore Purchases in Mosaic to recover an active subscription.
  2. Confirm that the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  3. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled from the Subscriptions page of your Apple Account.
  4. Generation packs are consumable purchases. In the current version, their balance is stored locally and may not be recoverable after uninstalling the App, erasing device data, or changing devices.

AI Data Permission

You can review or withdraw AI data-processing permission from Mosaic Settings. After withdrawal, no new generation request is sent to the AI service, while existing local creations remain available to view or delete.
